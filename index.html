How to read this

This is a screening estimate, not an inventory. Spend-based factors sit at the bottom of the GHG Protocol data-quality hierarchy. Use them to find hotspots and prioritise — not to report a final number or track reductions.
  • A spend-based factor gives every supplier in a sector the same intensity per euro, so a greener supplier and a dirtier one look identical. The ranking reflects where your money goes, not how clean each vendor is.
  • Once a category shows up as a hotspot here, replace it with supplier-specific or activity data for that category — that's the whole point of screening first.
  • EPA factors are US-economy averages in USD. For EU spend this is an approximation; EXIOBASE gives EU-specific factors and is the natural next upgrade.
  • Excludes Scope 1 & 2, and Scope 3 categories other than purchased goods/services (Cat 1) and capital goods (Cat 2).
Emission factors: U.S. EPA Supply Chain GHG Emission Factors v1.4 (USEEIO), public domain, kg CO₂e per 2024 USD purchaser price. This embedded set covers common SME spend buckets; the full set spans 1,016 NAICS-6 commodities and can be swapped in. Method follows the GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard, spend-based approach for Category 1.  ·  A screening instrument — figures are indicative.
